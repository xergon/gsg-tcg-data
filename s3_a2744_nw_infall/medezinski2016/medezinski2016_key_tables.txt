  cluster centre = R.A. 00:14:18.9, Decl. -30:23:22 (J2000), from Ebeling+2010;
  "closely corresponds to the cluster X-ray brightness peak".
COSMOLOGY (paper, Sec.1): Om=0.3, OL=0.7, H0=100h km/s/Mpc, h=0.7.
  Paper states 1 arcmin = 272 kpc at the cluster redshift  ->  4.533 kpc/arcsec.
  Independent recomputation at z=0.3072 gives 4.5277 kpc/arcsec. AGREE.
